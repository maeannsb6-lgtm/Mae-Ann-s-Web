## Approach #method
Seven steps: 01 Understand the business or operational process. 02 Map inputs, activities, decisions, bottlenecks and outputs. 03 Improve by removing unnecessary steps and redesigning. 04 Design the improved workflow and information flow. 05 Automate with AI, n8n, APIs and databases where they add value. 06 Validate logic and outputs. 07 Document the workflow, controls and system.
