## Inquiry handling SOP #process #documentation
Mae Ann wrote an SOP for how website inquiries are handled. Purpose: standardize how inquiries are captured, acknowledged, routed and tracked. Trigger: a visitor submits a validated inquiry form. Process: validate, store, notify, acknowledge, schedule follow-up, maintain status. Exceptions: spam or invalid data is rejected, backend failures are logged, and a direct-email fallback is provided. The automation lab flow is: website inquiry, Supabase, n8n, acknowledgement email, owner notification, follow-up workflow.
